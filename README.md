# Pattern-Reversal-Visual-Evoked-Potentials
VEP_DATASET_STANDARD3 contains 163 averaged transient pattern-reversal visual evoked potentials (105 normal, 58 pathological) from 67 adult subjects, 27 of whom have at least one pathological response. The 119 hospital responses from 38 subjects were recorded at the neurology department of the Mohammed VI University Hospital of Tangier on a Neuro-MEP-4 workstation. Recording used monocular stimulation, an occipital electrode referred to Fz or Cz, a 2–100 Hz band-pass, 10 kHz sampling and time-synchronous averaging of about 100 sweeps. Two protocols were used: A (49′ checks, 2 reversals/s) and B (24′–64′ checks, 1 reversal/s). The other 44 responses from 29 subjects come from a laboratory dataset and published sources; 36 of them were digitized from figures. All responses are resampled to 1 kHz and stored as 400 samples in µV. All 163 cover 0–249 ms, the main analysis window, and 140 cover the full 0–399 ms. For each response, the dataset records the subject, eye, protocol, signal type, published duration, label and label origin, plus sex and age when available. The label origin is one of: clinical report, healthy volunteer, diagnosis, P100 cursor or source document. Subjects are pseudonymized. The dataset is available from the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to approval by the CEHUT ethics committee for the clinical recordings.
